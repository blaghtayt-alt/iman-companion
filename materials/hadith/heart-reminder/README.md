# Heart Reminder: selected reports

All 36 protected Hadith reports below are available in Uzbek, Thai, Vietnamese, Japanese and Filipino. The reading text, necessary notes, selected sources and actual source conditions remain accessible with each report. This does not mean all Heart Reminder material is complete.

These readings received separate model-assisted review and are not human or scholarly certified. Source and reuse conditions are recorded; publisher notification and permission questions are not represented as universally resolved. The English descriptions are existing app editorial summaries, not scripture or verbatim translations. The references listed below identify the protected Arabic starting records. Selected translated reports can be distinct parallels, with their own narrators and references described in the source index.
