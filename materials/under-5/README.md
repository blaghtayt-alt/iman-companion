# Islam for children under five

Six teaching units for an adult to read with a young child, available in Arabic, English, French, Turkish, Indonesian, Malay, Brazilian Portuguese, Urdu, Bengali, Hindi, Russian, Spanish, Persian, German, Simplified Chinese, Uzbek, Thai and Vietnamese, each with **39 lessons**. Questions, responses, activities and pauses are retained.
