## Lesson 3: Allah made me

Allah made us...

And gave us our bodies...

Today we will look at something we use a lot...

Our hands...

Let's look at our hands together...

Let's gently open them...

Then gently close them...

And open them again...

...

Well done!...

Now let's move our fingers...

Let's try moving one finger...

Then let's try moving the other fingers...

...

Well done!...

What can we do with our hands?...

Let's think of one thing...

...

We hold a cup with our hands...

And we hold our toys...

So who made our hands...

And made us able to move them?...

...

Allah...

We also have eyes to see with...

Let's look at something near us...

What can we see?...

You can say what you see...

...

Well done!...

Allah made our eyes that we see with...

And our ears that we hear with...

And our tongues and lips that we speak with...

We did not make ourselves...

Before we were born...

We were in our mothers' tummies...

And Allah is the One who made our bodies...

Then we were born...

And we began to grow, little by little...

Our bodies are blessings from Allah...

A blessing is something good that Allah gives us...

Like our hands, our eyes, and our ears...

Now let's choose a part of our body...

A hand, an eye, or an ear...

What do we do with it?...

If we choose an eye...

And we say...

Allah made my eye...

...

Well done!...

Well done!...
