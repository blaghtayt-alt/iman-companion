## Lesson 17: When I do something wrong, I turn back to Allah

And wish we had not done it...

So what can we do now?...

Let's think...

...

We stop doing what is wrong...

And give the toy back to our friend...

Because it is their toy...

And we say to them...

I am sorry...

Let's practice saying it...

I am sorry...

...

Well done!...

And we do not want to take other people's things...

Without them saying we can...

And we ask Allah to forgive us...

O Allah, forgive me...

Let's say it together...

O Allah, forgive me...

...

Well done!...

We stop doing what is wrong...

...

We ask them...

Let's try saying this sentence...

...

Well done!...

And turn back to Allah...
