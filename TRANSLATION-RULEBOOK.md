# Faithful, natural Tadabbur translation

This rulebook supplements the existing source, independent-review, UI and book-publication rules. It does not authorize rewriting the Arabic, generating Quran translations, or bypassing any review or release hold.

1. Preserve the approved Arabic meaning exactly. Use natural target-language grammar and idiom, without adding sentences, ideas, explanations, imagery, certainty, chronology, causation or conclusions. Do not omit meaning to make wording smoother. Make the smallest justified correction; preserve everything outside its exact scope.
2. Distinguish literal awkwardness from an accurate technical term. Confirm the Arabic/context before changing anatomy, medicine, doctrine, negation, speaker, narrator, qualification or metaphor. For example, Arabic صفائح دموية means platelets; it must not be generalized to blood merely because a reviewer finds the specific term unusual. A literal rendering of a tongue expression may need idiomatic wording, but never apply a blanket tongue-to-mouth replacement.
3. Use the exact authorized published Quran translation selected for that language/edition. Do not generate or silently repair Quran wording. In the surrounding explanation, refer to the counterpart word used in that translation. Retain an Arabic/transliterated term only when the source's linguistic distinction genuinely cannot be conveyed without it, with its meaning immediately beside it. Do not invent an etymological or theological explanation.
4. Reuse verified Hadith and sayings from the language source bank, with their provenance, collection/number, exact variant, speaker and grading distinctions. An authorized parallel narration is usable only with its differences recorded; do not blend reports into one quotation. Keep generated-versus-published provenance in the bank, following existing app-display rules.
5. Preserve Quran/Hadith quotation boundaries, references, role/color coding, paragraph order, intentional removals, notes that convey actual meaning, and metadata. QC comments are not lesson content. Never restore deliberately removed Word items 283, 288, 311 or 316, or other recorded owner removals, merely because an older source or proposal includes them.
6. Bind every proposal to the current Arabic and target passage, edition, verse/block/span, original lecture/source evidence and file hashes. A prepared Arabic comparator is not authenticated lecture evidence. A missing or ambiguous match is a hold on that individual change, not permission to guess or delete a translation.
7. A different, actual Astra XHigh reviewer must explicitly record AGREE, DISAGREE or NEEDS_SOURCE for each proposed correction, with a brief fidelity reason. User approval of a batch is not a substitute for this source comparison. After any accepted change, the reviewer must check the resulting target text against the revised/controlling Arabic and perform a separate target-language-only readability reread of the affected context. Record the exact reviewed bytes/hash; a generic claim that a file was read is insufficient.
8. Apply only independently accepted, current-hash-matching corrections in safe versioned batches through the existing sole integration writer. Preserve before/after text and per-language decisions, do not create duplicate audits or interrupt active reviewers, and do not relabel an untranslated or unresolved passage as complete. Reuse the extended/simplified source bank instead of searching or translating quotations again.
9. Validate the actual effective app reader, source repository, quotations and color spans, not just a source file's existence. Carry affected books through BOOK-UPDATES.md and preserve its real Word/visual acceptance gates. Verify installed app bytes and actual public downloads/deployment separately. A source commit, private candidate or successful test is not a live release.
10. Respect the owner's current quota reserve and scoped queue. Al-Fatiha comes first for this correction batch; broader Quran auditing proceeds from a saved source map in later authorized scope, without duplicating already completed audit work or pretending its pending independent review has passed.
