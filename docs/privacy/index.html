Privacy Policy

Iman Companion
Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Last updated: 3 October 2026

Iman Companion is designed to minimize data collection. The app does not require an account for its core features and does not sell personal information.

1. Information we collect

Iman Companion does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, advertising ID, or device serial number for its core Quran, Hadith, memorization, adhkar, and learning features.

If you voluntarily use the in-app Send Feedback feature, the app may send the following information so the report can be understood and investigated:

  • the feedback category, such as bug, translation, content, suggestion, or other;
  • the message you choose to submit;
  • the current app screen and app language;
  • the app version;
  • Android version and device model;
  • a relevant Quran reference, such as Surah and Ayah, when the report concerns Quran content; and
  • the date and time of submission.

Please do not include personal or sensitive information in a feedback message.

2. How feedback data is used

Feedback data is used only for purposes such as diagnosing bugs, checking translation or content reports, improving app quality, and maintaining the app.

3. Firebase / Cloud Firestore

The feedback feature may use Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore to transmit and store submitted reports. Google may process standard technical and network information as necessary to provide and secure these services. Google's handling of data is governed by its own terms and privacy policies.

Firebase privacy and security information

4. Sharing of information

Iman Companion does not sell or rent user data. Feedback information may be processed by service providers used to operate the feature, such as Google Firebase, and may be disclosed if required by applicable law.

5. Analytics, advertising, and accounts

The current privacy design does not rely on advertising SDKs, third-party advertising profiles, or mandatory user accounts. If a future version adds analytics, crash reporting, sign-in, advertising, or another service that changes data handling, this policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Children and family use

Iman Companion includes content that may be suitable for families and children. The app does not intentionally ask children to provide personal information through its core features. Children should not enter their name, email address, phone number, location, school information, photographs, or other personal details into the feedback field. A parent or guardian should assist with any privacy-related request for a child.

7. Data retention and deletion

Feedback reports are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to review the report, improve the app, maintain security, or meet legal obligations. Reports that are no longer needed may be deleted.

For a privacy or deletion request, use the developer contact email shown on the Iman Companion Google Play store listing. For non-sensitive technical reports, you may also use the project's GitHub Issues page. Do not post private information in a public GitHub issue.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect data handled by the app. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International processing

Service providers used by the app may process data in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, data is handled according to the service provider's safeguards and applicable law.

10. Open-source software

Iman Companion is open source. The source repository is available at github.com/blaghtayt-alt/iman-companion. Open-source publication of the app's code does not make individual feedback submissions public.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

12. Contact

For privacy matters, use the developer contact email listed on the Iman Companion Google Play store page. For non-sensitive project questions, visit the Iman Companion GitHub repository.
