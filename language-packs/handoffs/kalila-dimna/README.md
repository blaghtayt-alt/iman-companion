# Kalila wa Dimna — downloadable chapters

All 15 language packs contain the reviewed edition through page 95:
**10 chapters, 95 full pages, and 95 separate thumbnails per language.**
Chapters 1–9 have ten pages each. Chapter 10 contains pages 91–95.
The story stops at page 95 for this release.

In the app, download your language pack from Downloads or Books. Books offers
your current interface language and Arabic for reading practice. Arabic, Urdu,
and Persian read right to left; other editions read left to right. Panels were
reordered for reading direction without mirroring the artwork.

## Verification and review coverage

- All 15 public ZIPs were downloaded. Archive, manifest, and every contained
  media file were checked against SHA-256 hashes.
- All 2,850 Kalila page/thumbnail files match the existing reviewed originals.
- All 15 editions passed browser installation, reload/persistence, and offline
  decoding of every page and thumbnail. The actual book reader, chapter 10 limit,
  reading direction, and interface-language/Arabic choices were checked.
- Original Arabic artwork and lettering retain their recorded production approval.
  Each translated edition has complete editorial AI review with recorded corrections
  resolved, mechanical checks on all 95 pages, and representative visual review.
  This does not claim separate full-size visual inspection of every translated page.
- The browser installer test used checksum-identical release files served locally,
  with network disabled for reading; this is not a physical-device APK test.

The per-language JSON files in this folder list every media path, actual image
dimensions, hashes, chapter/page ranges, direction, and precise review coverage.
Public download checks and offline reader results are in the verification files.
No new narration audio or new translation was produced in this delivery.
Existing other books and teaching audio remain in their respective packs.

Lightweight chapter/edition metadata stays in the base app. Full book media lives
only in downloadable language packs. The stable weekly catalogue remains at
[`language-packs/catalog.json`](../../catalog.json).
